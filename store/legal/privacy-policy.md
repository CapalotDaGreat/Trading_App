# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

TradeVision AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TradeVision AI mobile application. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** Email address, display name, and authentication provider data when you create an account.
- **Usage data:** App interactions, feature usage, and crash reports (if enabled in settings).
- **Device information:** Device type, operating system, and push notification tokens.
- **Subscription data:** Purchase history and subscription status via RevenueCat.
- **Trading preferences:** Watchlists, alerts, and settings you configure in the app.

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve the TradeVision AI service
- Send price alerts and AI insight notifications (with your consent)
- Process subscriptions and manage premium access
- Analyze anonymized usage patterns to improve the product
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage

Data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) and encrypted in transit. Push tokens and subscription status are synced to Firestore.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Firebase** — Authentication, database, and push notifications
- **RevenueCat** — Subscription management
- **Google AdMob** — Advertising (production builds only; disabled for premium users)

## Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting support@tradevision.ai. You can disable analytics and notifications in app Settings.

## Children's Privacy

TradeVision AI is not intended for users under 18 years of age.
