## 1. How to get help

1. Open the app and go to **Settings → Support** (in-app copy of this page).
2. Email **support@tradeacademy.cloud** from the address on your account when possible.
3. For privacy rights or deletion, use **Settings → Privacy** or email **privacy@tradeacademy.cloud**.
4. For suspected security issues, email **security@tradeacademy.cloud**.

Include: device OS, app version (Settings), whether you use Guest mode or a signed-in account, and steps to reproduce.

## 2. Common questions

### Account and Guest mode

- **Guest / local demo** explores Academy, Practice, Replay, Simulation, Journal, and Review on-device without cloud sync. It is not a Premium purchase path.
- **Signed-in accounts** may sync when Firebase is configured and you are online. Guest data does not automatically become a cloud account.

### Subscriptions (Aithera Pro)

- Billing is handled by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**.
- Use **Settings → Manage Subscription** (or the store’s subscription pages) to cancel or change plans.
- **Deleting your TradeAcademy account does not cancel store billing.**

### Notifications

- Study and training reminders are educational. They are **not** buy/sell alerts.
- You can disable push categories and respect quiet hours in **Settings → Notifications**.
- Background delivery may be delayed by the OS; Expo Go cannot provide production push behaviour.

### Simulation and scores

- Simulation uses labelled synthetic/sample data and default **USD 100,000** paper capital.
- Decision Quality Score (DQS) measures **process quality**, not price direction.
- Simulated P/L does **not** grade competence or certify live trading readiness.

### Technical troubleshooting

- Try signing out/in, clearing optional local caches only when prompted, or reinstalling for Guest-only issues.
- Native IAP, background alert evaluation, and production push require an **EAS Dev Client** or production build — not Expo Go.
