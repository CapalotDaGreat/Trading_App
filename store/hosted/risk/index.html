1. Educational and coaching tool only

TradeAcademy is a trading-education and simulated-practice application. It helps people learn concepts, practise chart reading and decisions, manage simulated capital, journal reasoning, and review process quality.

It is not:

  • personalised investment advice;
  • a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, short, or size any instrument;
  • a portfolio management or discretionary mandate service;
  • a broker, exchange, or payment/custody service;
  • a prediction of future prices, volatility, or returns.

Research Value Score (RVS) ranks whether an idea may deserve research time. Decision Quality Score (DQS) grades checklist/process completeness. Neither score predicts market direction or trading outcomes.

A 4+ / Everyone store rating is a content-suitability label. It is not permission to trade and not a statement that under-18 users may legally trade.
